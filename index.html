About the workshop

Overview

The objective of the workshop is to foster the fundamental research underlying lower bounds in quantum query complexity, one of the central tools for understanding the limits of quantum computation. It will begin with tutorials on the three principal lower bound techniques, followed by talks on recent advances that build on them. Our aim is to give participants a unified view of both the established machinery and the current research frontier, and to create space for discussion of the open questions that connect them.

  • Adversary method
  • Polynomial method
  • Compressed oracle method
